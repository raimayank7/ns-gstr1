GSTR-1 › Due date

GSTR-1 due date, QRMP and IFF

GSTR-1 has to be filed for every tax period, even when you sold nothing (then a nil return). Your buyers see your bills only after you file, so filing late also holds up their input tax credit.

The due dates (CGST Rule 59)

You fileDue date
Monthly GSTR-111th of the next month. October 2026 sales: by 11 November 2026.
Quarterly GSTR-1 (QRMP scheme)13th of the month after the quarter. July–September 2026: by 13 October 2026.
IFF (optional, QRMP only)From the 1st to the 13th of the next month, for B2B bills of the first and second month of the quarter.

The government sometimes extends a due date by notification. Check the GST portal (Services › Returns › Returns Dashboard) for your period.

QRMP and IFF in short

A business with an aggregate turnover of up to ₹5 crore in the last financial year can choose the QRMP scheme: GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B every quarter, tax paid monthly. In the first two months of the quarter it can upload B2B bills through the Invoice Furnishing Facility (IFF), up to ₹50 lakh of bill value a month, so that buyers get their credit sooner. Bills sent in IFF are not reported again in the quarterly GSTR-1.

In the maker, choose One quarter (QRMP) to see the whole quarter at once, or One month with a month of the quarter to prepare that month's IFF (use only its B2B and credit note tables).

Made a mistake? GSTR-1A and amendments

  • GSTR-1A (from the July 2024 period) lets you add or correct bills of the same period after filing GSTR-1 and before filing that period's GSTR-3B.
  • Later corrections go in the amendment tables (B2BA, B2CLA, B2CSA, CDNRA…) of a later GSTR-1. The time limit is 30 November after the end of the financial year, or the date of the annual return if earlier.
  • From the July 2025 period the tax liability that GSTR-1 puts into GSTR-3B can no longer be typed over in GSTR-3B, so the right place to correct sales is GSTR-1 or GSTR-1A.

Don't leave it for years

Since the Finance Act 2023, section 37(5) of the CGST Act says GSTR-1 cannot be filed after three years from its due date, and the portal now blocks such returns. Late returns also attract a late fee per day; check the current amount on the portal before you file.

A simple monthly routine

  1. By the 5th: check that every bill of the last month is saved (billing, cash memo, credit notes).
  2. Open the GSTR-1 maker, pick the month, correct everything in the warning box.
  3. Download the Excel, import it into the offline tool, upload the JSON.
  4. File by the 11th (or the 13th after the quarter), then file GSTR-3B.
