How to make a GSTR-1 summary here

  1. Make your bills here first. Tax invoices on billing.niveshsahayak.in, cash memos with GST, and credit or debit notes on credit-note.niveshsahayak.in are saved in this browser. This page reads them; you type nothing again.
  2. Choose the business and the period. Pick one month, or one quarter if you are in the QRMP scheme. Choose your turnover band so HSN codes are checked for the right number of digits.
  3. Read the warnings. Missing HSN codes, wrong buyer GSTINs, gaps in your number series and notes whose original invoice is not saved are listed at the top. Correct the bills on their own site, then press Read saved bills again.
  4. Check each table. B2B bills are listed one by one with the buyer GSTIN; small B2C sales are added up by state and rate; the HSN summary is split into B2B and B2C as the portal now asks.
  5. Download the files. Excel workbook gives one file with the offline tool's sheet names; All CSV files gives one file per table with the tool's own headers.
  6. Make the JSON and file. Import the files into the GST Returns Offline Tool, generate the JSON, upload it in GSTR-1 on the GST portal, check the summary there and file. See how to import into the offline tool.

What it does for you

  • Sorts every bill for you: by buyer GSTIN, state and bill value, into tables 4, 5, 6A, 7, 8, 9B, 11A, 12 and 13.
  • Uses the ₹1 lakh B2C Large limit for bills from the August 2024 period onwards, and the old ₹2.5 lakh for older bills.
  • Handles credit and debit notes: to registered buyers in 9B, and for small B2C bills it lowers or raises the B2CS figures, as the return expects.
  • HSN summary in two parts, B2B and B2C, with rate, quantity and unit codes (UQC) the portal accepts; services (SAC 99…) get no quantity.
  • Documents issued (table 13): each number series with first and last number and total, and a warning when numbers are skipped or repeated.
  • Offline tool layout: headers copied from the GST Returns Offline Tool V3.2.4 files, dates as DD-MMM-YYYY and states as "07-Delhi".

Your data stays with you

The summary is worked out in this browser from the bills saved in your Nivesh Sahayak data home (my.niveshsahayak.in). Nothing is sent to us or to the GST portal. You upload the return yourself, with your own login.

Check before you file. This is a working sheet made from your own bills. Bills made in other software, cancelled bills and amendments of earlier months are not in it. Your GSTR-1 is your responsibility; when in doubt, ask your accountant.

Questions people ask

Does this file my GSTR-1?

No. It prepares the figures and the files. You import them into the free GST offline tool, upload the JSON on the GST portal with your own login and file it there.

Where do the bills come from?

From the bills saved in this browser by the Nivesh Sahayak document sites (billing, cash memo, credit note, receipt and the rest). Bills from other software are not included.

Why is there no JSON download?

The GST portal's JSON format changes from time to time and only the official offline tool is sure to match it. So we give you the Excel and CSV in the offline tool's own layout and the tool makes the JSON.

Which bills go to B2C Large?

Bills to unregistered buyers in another state with a bill value above ₹1 lakh (from the August 2024 period; ₹2.5 lakh before). All other B2C bills are added up in B2C Small.

Can I use it for a quarterly return?

Yes. Choose "One quarter (QRMP)" and any month of the quarter; all three months are taken together.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free, with no login and no limit on bills.
